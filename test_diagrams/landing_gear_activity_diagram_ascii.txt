Safety Critical Points:
1. Airspeed check must be below 250 knots
2. Gear must be down and locked before landing
3. Emergency extension system must be available
4. Multiple verification points for gear position

Potential Hazards:
1. Gear extension at high speed
2. Gear not locked in position
3. Emergency extension system failure
4. False positive gear position indication
